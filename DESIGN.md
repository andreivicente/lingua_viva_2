## Brand & Style

This design system blends high-precision technical SaaS architecture with an editorial, humanistic warmth inspired by modern generative tooling and rigorous learning environments. Designed for sophisticated language acquisition and linguistics mastery, the aesthetic steers away from gamified, candy-colored motifs in favor of deliberate clarity, academic confidence, and understated luxury.

The style operates at the intersection of **Minimalism** and **Modern Enterprise Editorial**:
- **Warm Architectural Foundations:** Soft, parchment-tinted surfaces replace stark digital whites, establishing a tactile, book-like calm that minimizes eye strain during extended study sessions.
- **Micro-Precision Engineering:** Utilitarian, finely proportioned badges, monospaced metadata markers, and whisper-thin borders ground the experience in technical authority.
- **Deep Tone Contrast:** Crisp graphite and deep slate typography and accents punctuate the gentle canvas, delivering instant legibility and hierarchy.
- **Intentional Restraint:** Color is deployed strategically rather than decoratively, using deep pine teal and muted amber highlights to signal state, progression, and linguistic milestones without sensory overload.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by warm parchment neutrals (`#F4F1EA` canvas and `#FFFFFF` cards) contrasted against a near-black ink tone (`#141413`). This provides a print-like reading experience optimal for parsing complex syntax, vocabulary flashcards, and linguistic trees.

- **Primary (`#1A1A1A`):** Pure authority. Applied to primary CTAs, master headings, and prominent UI framing elements.
- **Secondary (`#245953`):** Deep editorial spruce. Serves as the intellectual anchor, distinguishing active module tabs, interactive nodes, selected phoneme cards, and completion indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706`):** Precision amber. Reserved for novelty markers, streaks, and focus callouts (`badge-highlight` paired with `badge-highlight-text`).
- **Neutral (`#F1EDE6`):** The contextual foundational layer across canvases, segmented controls, and nested sub-panels.

Borders rely on low-opacity ink overlays (`rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.08)`) instead of harsh gray hex values, allowing underlying warm tones to permeate the structure.

## Typography

The type system pairs **Space Grotesk** for display headers with **IBM Plex Sans** for running text, curriculum data, and technical UI controls. 

- **Space Grotesk** injects clean, architectural modernity with idiosyncratic geometric glyphs that mirror high-end engineering platforms without looking playful.
- **IBM Plex Sans** anchors readability through its humanist-grotesque hybrid nature, excelling at tabular vocabulary decks, phonetics, and dialogue transcripts.
- **JetBrains Mono** is introduced strictly for lexical syntax keys, phonetic notations (IPA), and curriculum status chips.
- Micro-labels and badge typography always leverage subtle positive letter spacing (`0.02em` to `0.06em`) in all-caps or medium-weight title casing to ensure razor-sharp rendering on dense learning dashboards.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model emphasizes airy, generous negative space to minimize cognitive burden and highlight complex language models.

- **Grid Architecture:** Desktop views conform to a 12-column responsive grid capped at a maximum container width of `1280px` for lesson workflows and `1440px` for multi-pane translation consoles. Mobile collapses to a single-column layout with strict vertical cadence.
- **Outer Canvas Rhythms:** Page-level margins begin at `3rem` (48px) on desktop, tapering to `1.25rem` (20px) on mobile viewports.
- **Component Padding Scale:** 
  - Standard cards utilize `space-xl` (40px) desktop and `space-md` (16px) mobile to achieve an open gallery feel.
  - Interactive chip pill rows and timeline markers utilize `space-xs` (4px) to `space-sm` (8px) internal gaps for grouping efficiency.
- **Vertical Air:** Major headline-to-content sections maintain `space-2xl` to `space-3xl` separation, letting primary thesis statements breathe naturally without competing UI elements.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy shadows, instead using flat architectural layering, tinted borders, and ultra-diffused atmospheric blurs:

1. **Surface Hierarchy:**
   - **Canvas Ground:** `#F4F1EA` warm matte base.
   - **Resting Panels:** `#FFFFFF` cards outlined with `1px solid rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.08)`.
   - **Active Floating Elements:** Pure white overlays with an ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -8px rgba(20, 20, 19, 0.05), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(20, 20, 19, 0.02)`.
2. **Glass & Translucency:**
   - Sticky navigation headers and floating practice toolbars use a frosted treatment: `background: rgba(244, 241, 234, 0.82)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and a subtle bottom border `rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.06)`.
3. **Compound Interactive Pill Depth:**
   - Segmented toggle decks (e.g., role-switch containers) employ a double-shell container: an outer ring in `#EBE6DD` holding inner interactive pills with soft `0 2px 4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)` shadows that mimic precision physical switches.

## Shapes

The geometric expression utilizes **Rounded (`2`)** geometry, balancing technical exactness with organic hospitality:

- **Interactive Pills & Badges:** Use full circular radius (`9999px`) for announcement chips, tag selectors, and primary action buttons, referencing the iconic dual pill toggles seen in technical AI environments.
- **Content Cards & Media Modules:** Standardize at `1rem` (16px) corner radiuses, generating defined, elegant rectangular modules.
- **Nested Inner Surfaces:** Inputs, small panels, and vocabulary breakdown lists use `0.5rem` (8px) corners to preserve proportional concentric harmony inside parent containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Dark obsidian background (`#1A1A1A`), crisp white text (`#FFFFFF`), full pill rounding (`9999px`), `12px 24px` padding. Transitions on hover to `#262626` with slight scale (`1.01`).
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background with subtle outer border (`1px solid rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.14)`), text `#1A1A1A`. Hover states reveal a warm tint (`rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.04)`).
- **Specialized Selector / Segmented Capsule:** Pill-shaped compound switch housing dual options (e.g., "Student" / "Educator" or "Phonetics" / "Script") with an active white pill card floating inside a warm neutral track.

### Chips & Badges
- **Announcement Pill:** Small caps `label-sm` badge featuring an accent pill container (`#F7E2B4`) with warm amber text (`#78350F`), paired with text links adorned with micro chevron icons.
- **Filter Tags:** Thin-bordered pills (`rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.12)`) featuring `#57534E` text. Active state transitions to `#245953` teal background with white text.

### Interactive Language Cards
- Built on `#FFFFFF` surfaces with fine `1px solid rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.08)` borders.
- Top section includes a monospace module index (`code-sm`, e.g., `MOD-04 // SYNTAX`) alongside an active status pip in `#0D9488`.
- Ample inner padding (`32px`), generous title spacing, and a bottom meta ribbon separated by a delicate hairline stroke.

### Input Fields
- Understated input envelopes with `#FFFFFF` background, `0.5rem` rounded corners, and a `1px solid rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.12)` border.
- On focus: crisp border color switch to `#245953` without harsh glowing rings—just a clean, hairline definition and subtle inner shadow.

### Dialogue & Transcript Lists
- Linear vertical sequences connected by low-contrast timeline indicators (`1px` dotted line in `rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.1)`).
- Audio playback controllers embedded as miniaturized, borderless circular icon nodes with high-contrast playback states.
